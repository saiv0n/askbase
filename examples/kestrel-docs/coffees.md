# Our coffees

All our coffees are bought directly from farms or small cooperatives we've visited, and we publish the price we pay per kilo on each product page.

## Current range

- Hollow Fern, Colombia, washed. Notes of red apple, panela and milk chocolate. Our house espresso. £9.50 for 250 g.
- Night Heron, Ethiopia, natural. Blueberry, jasmine and dark honey. Best as filter. £11.00 for 250 g.
- Long Field, Brazil, pulped natural. Hazelnut, cocoa and caramel. Low acidity, very forgiving. £8.50 for 250 g.
- Swift Decaf, Peru, sugarcane process decaf. Toffee and orange. £9.50 for 250 g.

Every coffee is also available in 1 kg bags at a 15% discount per gram.

## Caffeine and allergens

Swift Decaf is 99.9% caffeine free. All our coffees are roasted in a facility that handles no nuts, gluten or dairy. Our bags are compostable at home except for the valve, which goes in general waste.
