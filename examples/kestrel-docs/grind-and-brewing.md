# Grind sizes and brewing

## Which grind should I choose?

Whole bean stays fresh longest and is what we recommend if you have a grinder. If you don't, pick the grind that matches how you brew:

- Espresso: very fine.
- Moka pot: fine.
- Aeropress or V60 pour over: medium fine.
- Chemex or batch brewer: medium.
- French press or cold brew: coarse.

You can't change the grind of an order after it has been roasted, because we grind to order on roast day.

## How long does coffee stay fresh?

Whole bean is at its best between 7 and 30 days after the roast date printed on the bag. Ground coffee is best within 2 weeks. Keep it in the bag it came in, sealed, away from light and heat. Don't keep it in the fridge.

## Our basic pour over recipe

1. 15 g of coffee to 250 g of water, just off the boil (around 94°C).
2. Bloom with 40 g of water for 30 seconds.
3. Pour the rest slowly in circles, finishing by 1 minute 45 seconds.
4. Total brew time should be about 3 minutes. If it's faster and tastes sour, grind finer. If it's slower and bitter, grind coarser.
