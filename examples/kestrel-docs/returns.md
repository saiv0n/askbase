# Returns and refunds

## Our promise

If a coffee isn't for you, tell us within 30 days of delivery and we'll make it right, even if the bag is opened. Opened coffee can't be resold, so you don't need to send it back. Pass it to a friend instead.

## How to ask for a refund

1. Email hello@kestrel.example with your order number.
2. Tell us which coffee and, if you like, what you didn't enjoy about it. It helps us recommend something better.
3. Choose a refund to your original payment method or a replacement bag of a different coffee.

Refunds are issued within 2 working days of your email and usually show up on your statement 3 to 5 working days later.

## Equipment

Brewing equipment (grinders, kettles, drippers) can be returned unused and in its original packaging within 30 days. Return postage is free in the UK; outside the UK you pay return postage. Faulty equipment is covered by the manufacturer's warranty, which we handle for you for the first 12 months.

## Gift cards and subscriptions

Gift cards can't be refunded. For subscription refunds, see the subscriptions page.
