# Account and payment

## Payment methods

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Apple Pay, Google Pay and PayPal. Prices on the site are in pounds sterling and include UK VAT.

## Discount codes

Enter one discount code at checkout. Codes can't be combined with each other or with the subscription discount, and they can't be applied to an order after it's placed.

## Changing an order

You can change the address or cancel an order until it's roasted. Email hello@kestrel.example with your order number as soon as possible. Once an order has been roasted, we can't change it, but our returns promise still applies.

## Password and login

Use "Forgot password" on the login page to get a reset link by email. The link works for 1 hour. If it doesn't arrive within 10 minutes, check your spam folder or email us.

## Deleting your account

Email hello@kestrel.example from the address on your account and we'll delete your account and personal data within 30 days. We keep order records for 6 years because UK tax law requires it.
