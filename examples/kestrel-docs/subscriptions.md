# Subscriptions

## How it works

A subscription sends you freshly roasted coffee every 1, 2 or 4 weeks. You choose a single coffee, or "Roaster's choice", where we pick a different seasonal coffee each delivery. Subscribers save 10% on every bag and always get free UK shipping.

## Pausing, skipping and changing

You can pause, skip a delivery or change your coffee, grind or frequency from Account > Subscriptions at any time before 23:59 on the day before a roast day. Changes after that apply to the following delivery.

- Skip: skips only the next delivery.
- Pause: stops deliveries until you resume, for up to 6 months. After 6 months the subscription is cancelled automatically and we email you first.
- Cancel: there's no minimum term and no cancellation fee.

## Payment

Subscriptions are charged on the roast day of each delivery, not when you sign up. If a payment fails we try again after 2 days and email you. After a second failed attempt the delivery is skipped.

## Refunds for subscriptions

If you forgot to skip a delivery, tell us before it ships and we'll cancel it and refund you in full. After it ships, the normal 30 day returns promise applies.
