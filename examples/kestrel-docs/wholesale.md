# Wholesale for cafes and offices

We supply cafes, restaurants and offices across the UK. Wholesale prices start at 5 kg per order, and we deliver on our normal roast schedule.

## What you get

- Wholesale pricing from £22 per kg for Long Field up to £30 per kg for Night Heron.
- Free barista training for your team at our roastery in Bristol, or on site within 30 miles.
- Equipment advice and a loan grinder while yours is being repaired.

## Getting started

Email wholesale@kestrel.example with your business name, roughly how much coffee you use per week and how you brew. We reply within 2 working days and send free samples of 3 coffees.

Payment terms are 14 days from invoice for approved accounts.
