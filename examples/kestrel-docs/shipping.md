# Shipping and delivery

## Roast days and dispatch

We roast on Monday and Thursday. Every order ships within 48 hours of the next roast day, so your coffee is never more than a few days old when it leaves us. Orders placed after 10:00 on a roast day go into the following roast.

## Delivery times and costs

- United Kingdom: 1 to 2 working days with Royal Mail Tracked 48. Free on orders over £30, otherwise £3.95.
- European Union: 3 to 6 working days with DPD. £8.50 flat, free on orders over £60. Prices include import VAT, so there is nothing extra to pay on delivery.
- United States and Canada: 5 to 9 working days with DHL. £14 flat.
- We don't ship to PO boxes or to the rest of the world yet.

## Tracking your order

You get a tracking link by email as soon as the parcel is handed to the carrier. If 3 working days pass after that with no movement on the tracking page, email hello@kestrel.example and we'll chase the carrier for you.

## Lost or damaged parcels

If a parcel arrives damaged or is marked delivered but you can't find it, tell us within 14 days. We'll send a replacement or a full refund, whichever you prefer. A photo of the damage helps but isn't required.
